# Dominion Expansions Rank (bluekms21)

- The base game and all 16 official expansions, graded on how they felt to me at the table
- 🏅 S to D are expansions I have played; Unplayed means not yet (no Korean edition, or never got a copy)
- 🇰🇷 Korean release = Korea Boardgames edition, as of 2026-10-21
- Images are each expansion's BGG cover


# global
+ 📝 Victory cards in the Supply usually bring a unique mechanism and give everyone a plan B. I like to include at least one kind every game. It keeps strategies from calcifying and gives the players in second place a way back in.

# Dominion - Intrigue.jpg
- 🏅 My grade: A
- 🇰🇷 Korean release: Yes
- ⭐ BGG rating: 7.69
- 🧠 Weight: 2.41 / 5
+ 👥 Players: 2 ~ 6 (3 best)
+ 📅 Year: 2009
+ 🔤 Korean title: 도미니언: 장막 뒤의 사람들
+ 💬 It was meant to ship alongside the base game, and the cards are just as intuitive and strong.
+ 💬 Many cards carry two effects at once. Given how quickly base-game strategies calcify, I sometimes think it should have shipped in the same box.
+ 💬 Masquerade is a fun, memorable card, but depending on seating you often wish you could pass the other way, so in some games we house-ruled it so the passer picks the direction.

# Dominion - Renaissance.jpg
- 🏅 My grade: A
- 🇰🇷 Korean release: Yes
- ⭐ BGG rating: 8.28
- 🧠 Weight: 2.43 / 5
+ 👥 Players: 2 ~ 4 (3 best)
+ 📅 Year: 2018
+ 🔤 Korean title: 도미니언: 르네상스의 도래
+ 💬 Villagers are the heart of this set. They keep your deck moving even without a +Action card in hand, which takes away much of the frustration of a bad draw.
+ 💬 Artifacts are good too. Racing for the single copy adds tension to a game that is otherwise light on interaction.
+ 💬 Projects add to the appeal without slowing the game.
+ 🔗 https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgameexpansion/257673/dominion-renaissance
+ thumbnail-url: https://cf.geekdo-images.com/A8eKS09rQjkuuKHtzPmVhA__previewthumb/img/TIy_RyelUopqXt-Y5TbxGtG-Nlc=/fit-in/300x320/filters:strip_icc()/pic4250088.jpg
